# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- **Continue.** The title screen offers to continue from the day you left. It still says you left them.
- **Brightness check** on first launch: turn it up until you can barely see the third mug.
- **Streamer mode** in Settings. The desktop build no longer asks "Are you sure?" before it opens, and only says your name once.
- **A shorter drive in.** The prologue reaches the choice in under half a minute, and something is wrong in the back seat within the first ten seconds.
- **Fewer words on the walls.** Wednesday has nine pieces of writing instead of eighteen, and none of them are picked at random any more. Two of them change while you are not looking.
- **The objective goes quiet from Wednesday.** It only appears if you are stuck, or when it lies.
- **A repeated day tells you why** the moment you wake up.
- **Every day ends differently.** Tuesday cuts to silence, Wednesday crashes, Thursday goes white and Friday comes up out of the white.
- **Scares no longer take the camera.** They wait behind you until you turn around.
- **No buttons at the end.** She asks once and the exit comes up on the right. Pull over, or keep driving.
